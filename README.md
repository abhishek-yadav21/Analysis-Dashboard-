# Analysis-Project
A collection of data analysis projects built to uncover insights through structured reporting and interactive dashboards.

# 1. Monthly Department Budget Analysis
Tool: Microsoft Excel

Period: Q1 2025 (January – March)
# Description
This project analyzes budget vs actual spending across 5 departments — Finance, HR, IT, Marketing, and Sales — for the first quarter of 2025. It tracks where money was planned to go and where it actually went, using pivot tables, variance calculations, and a summary dashboard.
- Dashboard Image- <a href="https://github.com/abhishek-yadav21/Analysis-Dashboard-/blob/main/Monthly%20department%20budget%20dashboard%20screenshot.png">view Dashboard image</a>
  
# Key Insights

- Total budget was exceeded by ₹68,000 — actual spend came in at ₹11,54,000 against a planned ₹10,86,000

- Sales was the biggest overspender — went ₹60,000 over budget, accounting for 88% of the total variance

- HR was the only department under budget — saved ₹2,000 against its allocation

- Finance stayed exactly on budget — zero variance across all 3 months

- Spending grew every month — from ₹3.68L in Jan to ₹3.80L in Feb to ₹4.06L in Mar

- March revenue beat its target — actual revenue of ₹1,90,000 exceeded the budget of ₹1,70,000 by ₹20,000

# 2. Retail Sales Dashboard
Tool: Power BI
Type: Interactive Dashboard
# Description
This Power BI dashboard gives a complete view of retail sales performance. It tracks total sales and profit, and breaks them down by product category, customer segment, and region — making it easy to spot what's selling, who's buying, and where growth is coming from.
# Key Insights

- Electronics, Furniture, and Office Supplies are the three core product categories tracked for revenue contribution

- Sales by Region covers East, West, North, and South — helping identify high and low performing areas

- Three customer segments — Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office — allow comparison of buying behavior across different buyer types

- Quantity sold by customer is tracked to understand volume trends alongside revenue

- KPI cards for Total Sales and Total Profit give an instant snapshot of overall business health
